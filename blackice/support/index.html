Requirements

Black ICE runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 27.0 or later, including iPhone Duo.

Playing on two devices

Each player needs Black ICE on their own iPhone or iPad, signed in to Game Center (Settings › Game Center). Nobody needs an iPhone Duo. In the game, go to TWO CREWS, TWO PHONES and choose:

  • INVITE to play a friend anywhere. Pick them in Game Center, and they get an invitation. You both need an internet connection.
  • NEARBY to play someone in the same room. Both of you choose NEARBY, and the game finds the other device.

Then deploy your ICE. The match starts when you are both ready.

It says Game Center is switched off

  • Sign in to Game Center in Settings › Game Center, then come back to the game.
  • If it says playing other people is switched off, check Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions › Game Center › Multiplayer Games.

NEARBY does not find the other device

  • Choose NEARBY on both devices at about the same time. Each one only looks while its Game Center screen is open.
  • Keep the devices close together, in the same room, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on.
  • Check that Nearby Players is on for both of you: Settings › Game Center › Nearby Players.
  • Try INVITE instead. Game Center's invite screen lists players nearby as well as your friends.
  • Make sure both devices have the latest version of Black ICE from the App Store.
  • If you use a VPN, try turning it off.

My friend did not get the invitation

  • Check you are Game Center friends, and that they are signed in to Game Center with notifications allowed for Game Center.
  • Ask them to open Black ICE. An invitation can also be accepted from inside the game.

The invitation also appeared on my own device

Game Center sends invitations through Messages. If your device is also signed in to the other player’s Messages account, for example an iPad shared in a family, the invitation shows up on it too. Accept it on the device you invited, not the one that sent it.

The connection dropped in the middle of a match

A short drop mends itself. Keep Black ICE open on both devices, and no moves are lost: each one is resent until the other device has it.

If the link is lost for longer, the game says LINK LOST. One of you sees a RECONNECT button, which sends the other player a Game Center invitation. The other is asked to wait for it. Accept the invitation, and the match carries on where it stopped.

If the other player does not come back, either of you can WITHDRAW to end the match.

A match on two devices is not saved. If either player closes Black ICE, the match ends and cannot be picked up again, so start a new one.

If the other player switches to another app, you see that they STEPPED AWAY. The match waits for them to come back.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to play against the computer, or with two people sharing one device. An INVITE match with a friend elsewhere needs the internet.

Do I need an account?

No account with us. To play against someone on their own device, you need to be signed in to Game Center.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. It is not synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

I closed the app in the middle of a match. Is it lost?

Not if it was against the computer or two people on one device. Those are saved as you play, and you can pick one up again with Resume Match the next time you open the game.

A match against someone on their own device is not saved, so closing the app ends it.

Does the game collect any data about me?

No. See our privacy policy.
